MEME — MARK & ELKE

Where words meet music.

MEME is the Belgian music and poetry project of Mark & Elke. They bring original poetic texts and music together in an intimate live performance that moves between tenderness, humour and melancholy.

Based near Ghent (Gent), Belgium, MEME creates original music, poetry and spoken-word performance. Their work explores love and loss, memory and desire, tenderness and the small absurdities of everyday life.

For MEME, words, music and performance belong to the same story. The result is an original live experience that can move from intimate and reflective to playful, humorous and unexpected.

MEME — Mark & Elke performs in Belgium at concerts, cultural events, literary gatherings, gardens, parties and other intimate live settings.
